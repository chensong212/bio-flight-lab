## Overview / 概述

研制仿生变体滑翔机平台，通过翼面形状连续变化实现抗阵风飞行。核心包括：电液人工肌肉驱动、曲率本体感知、扰动观测器与在线变弯度控制。目标是在低频、静音、分布式驱动的约束下实现稳定的变体飞行。

We are building a bio-inspired morphing glider that uses continuous wing-shape change for gust rejection. Key components: electrohydraulic artificial muscle actuation, curvature-based proprioception, disturbance observer, and online camber control. The goal is stable morphing flight under low-frequency, silent, and distributed actuation constraints.
