## Overview / 概述

研制具备仿生栖息能力的无人机平台。仿照鸟类在树枝/墙壁上的快速着陆与附着行为，设计轻量化抓取机构与视觉引导着陆策略，使无人机能在非结构化环境中自主选择并停靠于高位栖息点，大幅延长任务续航。

We are developing a bio-inspired perching drone. Mimicking the rapid landing and attachment behavior of birds on branches and walls, the project designs lightweight grasping mechanisms and vision-guided landing strategies for autonomous perching in unstructured environments, significantly extending mission endurance.
