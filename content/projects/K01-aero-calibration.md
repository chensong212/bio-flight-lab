## Overview / 概述

建立一套从风洞实验到 CFD 仿真的扑翼气动力标定流程。核心目标：对三维刚性扑翼在可控运动学参数下的周期平均与相位分辨气动响应进行系统性标定，为 ROM 建模提供可靠数据基础。

We are building a systematic calibration pipeline for flapping-wing aerodynamic forces, from wind-tunnel experiments to CFD simulations. The goal is to produce reliable cycle-averaged and phase-resolved data across a wide parameter space for reduced-order modeling.
